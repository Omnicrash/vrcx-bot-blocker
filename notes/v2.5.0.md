## What's new in 2.5.0

- **VRCNext.** Bot Blocker now works in VRCNext too. The Windows setup installs it in VRCX and in VRCNext, whichever you have. In VRCNext, open it from Tools > Bot Blocker.
- **New name.** VRCX Bot Blocker is now VRC Bot Blocker.
- **Empty bio.** A new rule for players without a bio. It's off by default.
- **Warnings** now cover any two of Visitor rank, Empty bio and a new account: together they fit almost every new player.
- The bio rule is now labeled with every field it reads: bio, links, pronouns and status.
