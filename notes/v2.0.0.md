## What's new in 2.0

**Shared blocklist.** Bots that other users run into get blocked for you too, often before they
reach you. Every report is checked on VRChat by the server before anything is listed, and a person
looks over the list every day. Real people who were caught by mistake are taken off for everyone.
You can switch it off in the installer or on the Bot Blocker page.

**Smarter rules.** A player is now blocked once at least 2 of the checked rules fit, instead of all
of them. By default that's 2 of a default name, the Robot avatar and an account under a week old.
You set the number on the Bot Blocker page. Every rule can be set to Always block, and the bio rule
takes your own list of words and sites. Visitor rank and Nuisance rank are off by default now.

**Recommended settings.** When you update, the installer offers to put your rules back to the new
defaults. It's ticked by default, and it's recommended for this update.

**Automatic updates.** From now on Bot Blocker updates itself. A new version runs from the next
time VRCX starts. You can switch this off on the Bot Blocker page.

**Accounts that no longer exist** are taken off the shared list.
