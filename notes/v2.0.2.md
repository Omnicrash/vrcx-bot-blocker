## What's new in 2.0.2

- **Restart** in the update notice restarts VRCX. Before, it only closed VRCX.
- The Bot Blocker page shows when an update is ready, with a **Restart** button.
- **Check for updates** on the Bot Blocker page looks for a new version right away.
- Bot Blocker checks for updates as soon as VRCX starts.
